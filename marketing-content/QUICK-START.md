## Mix-and-Match Components

### Power Openers (Choose One)

1. **Question**: "Still [suffering from problem]?"
2. **Bold Statement**: "[Product] changed everything."
3. **Before/After**: "Two weeks ago, I [had problem]. Today, I [have solution]."
4. **Statistics**: "[X]% of people struggle with [problem]."
5. **Story**: "Meet [Name]. She [faced problem]. Then she discovered [product]."

### Benefit Bullets (Use 3-5)

Format: `✓ [Specific outcome] + [Timeframe/Number]`

Examples:
- ✓ Reduce wrinkles by 73% in 14 days
- ✓ 40-hour battery life (charge once a week)
- ✓ Supports up to 350 lbs with 10-year warranty
- ✓ Removes 99.9% of contaminants instantly
- ✓ Free shipping + arrives in 3-5 days

### Social Proof Snippets (Choose One)

1. **Review Count**: "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Over [X,XXX] five-star reviews"
2. **Testimonial**: '"[Short powerful quote]" - [Name], Verified Buyer'
3. **Statistics**: "[XX]% of customers report [specific result]"
4. **Customer Count**: "Join [X,XXX]+ happy customers"
5. **Rating**: "[X.X]/5.0 stars | [X,XXX] reviews"

### Urgency Triggers (Choose One)

1. **Countdown**: "Sale ends in [X] hours"
2. **Stock**: "Only [X] left at this price"
3. **Deadline**: "Offer expires [Day/Date]"
4. **Limited Edition**: "Limited first batch of [X] units"
5. **Seasonal**: "Black Friday pricing ends tonight"

### Call-to-Action Phrases (Choose One)

1. **Direct**: "Shop Now" | "Buy Now" | "Order Today"
2. **Benefit-Focused**: "Start [Desired Outcome]" | "Get [Benefit]"
3. **Risk-Free**: "Try Risk-Free" | "Test for [X] Days"
4. **Urgency**: "Claim Your Discount" | "Don't Miss Out"
5. **Transformation**: "Transform Your [X]" | "Upgrade Your [X]"

## Common Customizations by Product Type

### Physical Products
Add:
- Shipping timeframe
- Dimensions/size options
- Material/construction details
- Color options
- Weight capacity (if applicable)

### Digital Products
Add:
- Instant access mention
- Device compatibility
- Format (PDF, video, audio, etc.)
- Downloadable resources included
- Lifetime access vs. subscription

### Subscription Products
Add:
- Billing frequency
- What's included each period
- Cancel anytime policy
- First box/month details
- Subscription length options

### Services
Add:
- Turnaround time
- What's included/process
- Qualifications/credentials
- Communication method
- Booking/scheduling info

## Troubleshooting Quick Fixes

**Problem: Not getting clicks**
→ Add stronger urgency (countdown, limited stock)
→ Make discount bigger/more visible
→ Add more social proof

**Problem: Not getting conversions**
→ Simplify your offer
→ Make guarantee more prominent
→ Reduce friction (easier checkout)

**Problem: Not getting engagement**
→ Ask more questions
→ Use more relatable examples
→ Show more personality/emotion
